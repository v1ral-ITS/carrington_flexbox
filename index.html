Reflection

The responsive card row was the hardest layout for me to understand because the cards needed to share space on a wide screen and move onto new rows on a narrow screen. I looked up how flex-wrap works with the flex shorthand, especially how the starting size affects when a card wraps. The navigation example helped me understand that space-between separates the logo from the links, while align-items keeps them vertically aligned. The hero showed me why the container needs a minimum height for vertical centering to be visible. Outside class, I have seen navigation bars on shopping websites, centered hero headlines on software product homepages, and responsive card rows in online project portfolios. These examples helped me connect the CSS properties to layouts I regularly use.
